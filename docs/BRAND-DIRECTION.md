# Brand Direction

## Positioning
**Premium aquatic performance with human coaching.**

The brand should feel disciplined, calm, modern and confident — not childish, noisy or generic.

## Palette direction
- Deep ocean navy — primary atmosphere
- Pool cyan — action / motion accent
- Clear white — readability and trust
- Cool slate — secondary surfaces

Final color tokens wait for approved logo assets.

## Visual system
- Water refraction and caustic light as a restrained motif
- Large editorial typography
- Real swimming photography whenever available
- Fluid section transitions
- Controlled glass surfaces
- Minimal icon language
- Strong negative space

## Motion
Motion represents water: drift, ripple, reveal, buoyancy.
Never use motion merely because it is possible.

## Arabic
Arabic is a first-class layout, not a mirrored English page.
Test typography, line length, numerals, CTA labels and mixed Arabic/English terms independently.

## Photography
Prefer:
1. real coach,
2. real pool,
3. real training,
4. progress moments,
5. parent-approved student stories.

Reject generic stock imagery when it could be mistaken for academy proof.
