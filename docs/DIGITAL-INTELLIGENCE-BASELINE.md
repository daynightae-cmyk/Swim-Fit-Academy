## Evidence language
- **VERIFIED** — direct or high-confidence evidence.
- **SUPPORTED** — multiple independent signals but not absolute.
- **UNVERIFIED** — plausible but not established.
- **UNAVAILABLE** — source access prevented verification.
- **NOT FOUND** — active search produced no matching evidence.

Do not collapse those states into one another.
