# Product Foundation

## Mission
Build the most credible, elegant and conversion-efficient digital experience for Swim Fit Academy in Abu Dhabi.

## Primary user
A parent or adult swimmer who wants to know: who teaches, where, when, how much, whether the academy is trustworthy, and how to start.

## Primary conversion
WhatsApp inquiry / trial-class request.

## Product priorities
1. Truthful business data.
2. Local discovery.
3. Mobile speed.
4. Trust proof.
5. Bilingual clarity.
6. Conversion measurement.
7. Visual distinction.

## Non-goals for v1
- No unnecessary admin panel.
- No database without a real workflow.
- No heavy WebGL in the critical path.
- No fake counters, reviews, awards or student totals.
- No complex booking engine unless operations require one.

## UX promise
Every visitor should answer the five purchase questions within one short scroll: **What? Who? Where? When? How do I start?**

## Definition of done
A production URL is not “done” until build, tests, mobile QA, accessibility, SEO, analytics, CTA tracking and visual regression checks are all evidenced.
